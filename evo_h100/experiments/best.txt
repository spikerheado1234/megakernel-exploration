Summary
-------
* best/triton: wins every shape. Smallest win 1.01x at B=4, N=640 (where
  Triton happens to land a fast schedule on a non-power-of-two N), largest
  1.78x at B=8, N=1024.
* best/prod-tk: wins every shape, 1.13x – 1.28x. Roughly a uniform ~15-20 %
  lift over the shipping kernel.
* At the largest shape we measure (B=8, N_CTX=1024) the best kernel hits
  96.65 TFLOP/s, ~9.8 % of H100 BF16 peak (~989 TFLOP/s). The remaining
  gap to peak is the inherent overhead of EvoAttention vs vanilla MHA:
  pair-bias load + add and res-mask load + add are on top of the two
  attention matmuls and are not counted in our FLOP measure.

The kernel sitting in best.cu is the v6_one_sem.cu kernel from the
experiments sweep. The path to it is documented in log.md.
